Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

GradeWise collects minimal student information required to process and store academic performance records:

  • Student Profile Identifiers: Name, Roll Number (e.g. 23A31A6201), Admission Category (Regular or Lateral Entry), Branch/Department, Phone Number, and hashed Password.
  • Technical Logs: IP address and HTTP access timestamps for security audit purposes.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used exclusively to provide academic progress tracking services, including SGPA and CGPA credit-weighted calculations.

3. Academic Records

Academic records (subject codes, titles, credit values, earned grades, and backlog flags) are entered by students or matched from published university gazettes.

4. Authentication & Security

All student requests require cryptographically verified JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Access to student records is scoped strictly to the authenticated user's Roll Number.

5. Data Sharing & AI Processing

GradeWise does not sell, rent, or trade student personal information or grade records with third-party advertisers or commercial entities.

When an authenticated student uses the GradeWise AI Assistant, minimal non-sensitive academic summary metrics (such as branch, semester SGPAs, and calculated CGPA) are processed securely via Groq API infrastructure to answer your academic queries. Sensitive credentials (passwords, JWT secrets, reset tokens) are never transmitted to AI providers.

6. Data Retention

Your student profile and associated semester records are retained for as long as your account remains active.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your personal account and academic history at any time.

8. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Help & Support Center.
