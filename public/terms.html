By accessing or using GradeWise, you agree to comply with these Terms of Use. GradeWise provides academic progress tracking tools for engineering students following university credit regulations.

2. Student Accounts

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. Registration requires accurate student profile details including your roll number.

3. Academic Information

GradeWise stores user-provided subject codes, credits, and earned grades to construct semester performance histories.
