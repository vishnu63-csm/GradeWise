PRODUCT STORY

Built around a simple problem.

B.Tech students often maintain semester results across PDFs, screenshots, spreadsheets and notes. GradeWise brings those academic records into one structured view.

What GradeWise Does

GradeWise provides a dedicated application where engineering students can store their semester results, compute exact credit-weighted Semester Grade Point Averages (SGPA), and monitor Cumulative Grade Point Averages (CGPA) throughout their degree.

By organizing semester history in a persistent digital dashboard, students eliminate manual calculation errors and always know their current standing.

Designed for B.Tech Students

B.Tech degree programs involve complex credit structures across multiple semesters, including lab credits, theory credits, elective weightings, and lateral entry rules.

GradeWise supports semester-wise subject codes, credits, letter grades, and backlog tracking under university regulations such as JNTUK R23, giving students full clarity over passed credits and overall degree progress.

Built Around Three Principles

Our product architecture is governed by three fundamental principles:

Clarity

Academic performance should be easy to understand with structured tables and trend curves.

Accuracy

Calculations should follow the defined university grading and credit-weighting structure.

Privacy

Student academic information should be handled securely with authenticated access scoping.
