About GradeWise

Your academic performance, results, and progress — organized in one intelligent platform.

GradeWise is an academic performance management platform designed to help students understand, track, and manage their academic journey in one place.

Instead of manually calculating SGPA, CGPA, percentage, credits, and academic progress across multiple semesters, GradeWise brings everything together into a clear and organized dashboard.

Students can maintain their academic records, monitor semester-wise performance, identify backlogs, track improvement, and access institution-published results automatically when they are available.

GradeWise is built to make academic information easier to understand, accessible, and useful for every student.
