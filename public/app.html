🧮 SGPA Calculation ▼

SGPA is calculated for a single semester using the weighted average of credit points:

SGPA = Σ (Credit × Grade Point) / Σ Credits
Full precision maintained internally — rounded only for display

Example: A subject with 3 credits and grade A (9 points) contributes 27 credit-points. The sum of all credit-points divided by total credits gives the SGPA.

📈 CGPA Calculation ▼

CGPA is the cumulative weighted average across all applicable semesters:

CGPA = Σ (Semester Credits × SGPA) / Σ Semester Credits
Uses exact/unrounded SGPA values internally
💯 Percentage Conversion (R23) ▼

The official JNTUK R23 percentage conversion formula:

Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10
Also applied per-semester: Semester % = (SGPA − 0.75) × 10

Example: CGPA of 8.50 → Percentage = (8.50 − 0.75) × 10 = 77.50%

🎓 Regular Entry vs Lateral Entry ▼
Regular Entry

Applicable semesters: 1-1, 1-2, 2-1, 2-2, 3-1, 3-2, 4-1, 4-2

Roll number format: XX HP 1A XXXX (5th–6th chars: 1A)

Lateral Entry

Applicable semesters: 2-1, 2-2, 3-1, 3-2, 4-1, 4-2

Semesters 1-1 and 1-2 are excluded from CGPA calculation.

Roll number format: XX HP 5A XXXX (5th–6th chars: 5A)
