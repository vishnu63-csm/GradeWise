Frequently Asked Questions

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) measures your academic performance in a single semester. GradeWise calculates SGPA by multiplying each subject's credit weighting by its earned grade point, summing the total points, and dividing by total credits in that semester.
Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) represents your cumulative performance across all completed semesters. GradeWise uses official university credit weighting. Under JNTUK R23 guidelines, percentage is calculated as Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10.
When your profile category is set to "Lateral Entry", GradeWise automatically excludes 1-1 and 1-2 semester credits from CGPA and overall percentage calculations according to university academic rules.
